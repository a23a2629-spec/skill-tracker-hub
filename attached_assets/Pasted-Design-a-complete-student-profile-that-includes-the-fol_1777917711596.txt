Design a complete student profile that includes the following modules:
1. Personal Information

2. Identification Details
Include IC/Passport, student ID, and verification status.
3. Contact Information
Include phone numbers, email, and address.
4. Emergency Contact
Include guardian or emergency contact details.
5. Academic Background
Include previous education, certificates, results, and achievements.
6. University Program Details

7. Enrollment Information
Include registration status, advisor, and campus details.
8. Academic Performance
Include GPA, CGPA, attendance, and subject records.
9. Financial Information
Include income declaration, sponsorship, and payment status.
10. Family Background
Include household details, parental occupation, and dependency status.
11. Health Information
Include medical conditions, allergies, disability support needs, and insurance.
12. Mental Health Support Module
Include counseling requests and support tracking (high privacy).
13. Accommodation Details
Include hostel or housing information.
14. Skills & Interests
Include technical skills, soft skills, and career goals.
15. Disciplinary Records
Include warnings, violations, and case status.
16. Documents Section
